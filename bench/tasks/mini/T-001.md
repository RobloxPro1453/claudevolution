## Goal

`slugify` in `src/text.js` must produce URL slugs: lowercase, words joined by a single `-`, and only the characters `a-z`, `0-9` and `-` kept. Leading and trailing dashes are removed.

Examples: `"Hello, World!"` → `"hello-world"`, `"  Mini  Proje 2 "` → `"mini-proje-2"`.
