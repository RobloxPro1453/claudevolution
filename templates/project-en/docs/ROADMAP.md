# Roadmap

The source of work is a **concrete list**: no open-ended "scan, find, fix" items. Each item can be described on its own and its completion can be seen by eye or by a test. A finished item becomes `[x]`, it is not deleted. The order may change on evidence; the reason goes into a `docs/PROGRESS.md` entry.

What is being looked at now, and why (2-3 lines).
