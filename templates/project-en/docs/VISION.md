# Vision

This document belongs to the owner. Agents do not change the goal; they write suggestions in `docs/ROADMAP.md`. The `cv-init` skill fills this page by talking with the owner.

## Goal

<Two or three sentences: what we are making, who will use it, what the owner can do when it is finished.>
Example: "A desktop app that tracks the shop's stock and sales on one screen. Only I will use it; it must work without internet."

## The owner's decisions

Dated, one line each; newest on top. When a decision changes, do not delete the old one, strike it through.

- {{date}}: <decision> (why: <one sentence>)

## Finish line

All are needed to say "done"; write them measurably.

- [ ] <e.g. "A day's sales entry takes 3 clicks">
- [ ] <e.g. "Opens in under 2 seconds with 100 thousand records">

## Feel and quality measure

The number of tasks does not measure quality. Define from day one what will make the owner say "well done", and write how it will be tested.

- <e.g. "Blind test: side by side with the reference app's screen, you cannot tell at first sight which one is ours">
- <e.g. "My mother can enter the first record without a manual">

## Invariants

Things that must never break whatever happens (AGENTS.md > Hard rules is their checkable form).

- <e.g. "The user's data is never deleted silently.">
