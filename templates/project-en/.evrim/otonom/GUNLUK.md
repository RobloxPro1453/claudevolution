# Journal (appended, never deleted; only the last 10 lines are read at the start of a cycle)

Each cycle 3-6 lines, plain and honest: what I did, what it cost (quota %, $), was it worth it, what next time. A sleep cycle is one line. Entries are added by `.evrim/cv cycle end`; you may add by hand too.
